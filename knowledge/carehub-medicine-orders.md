# Ordering medicines on CareHub

## How ordering works
Browse pharmacies or search for a medicine, add items to your cart and check out with a delivery address (or choose store pickup), your phone number and a payment method. You can order as a guest or while signed in; signed-in patients can see their orders under **Order History**.

## Prescription medicines
Some medicines need a prescription. For those, upload a clear photo or PDF of the prescription at checkout; the order cannot be placed without it. The AI assistant cannot place prescription-medicine orders — use the cart and checkout on the Medicines page.

## Prices, stock and delivery
Prices and stock come from the pharmacy and are checked again when you place the order. If the price changed or an item sold out you will be asked to refresh. Delivery fees and delivery times differ by pharmacy and are shown on its page.

## Order statuses
Placed, confirmed, preparing, out for delivery and delivered. An order can also be cancelled by the pharmacy or CareHub.

## Payment
CareHub records the payment method you choose (for example cash on delivery), but it does not process card or mobile-wallet payments online at the moment. Pay the pharmacy as arranged at delivery or pickup.

## Changes and cancellations
The app does not yet let customers cancel an order themselves. To change or cancel an order, contact the pharmacy or CareHub support as soon as possible, before the order is out for delivery.
