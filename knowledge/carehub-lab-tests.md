# Booking lab tests on CareHub

## How to book
Open Labs, choose your city and a laboratory, select one or more tests and pick a date, a time slot and how the sample is collected: **home collection** (where the lab offers it for that test) or **visiting the lab**. For home collection you must give an address. You can book while signed in or as a guest, but signing in lets you track bookings under **My Lab Tests**.

## Prices
Each test shows its price in rupees. The booking total is the sum of the selected tests. Compare the same test across laboratories before booking.

## Statuses
Pending, confirmed, sample collected, report ready, or cancelled.

## Cancelling
You can cancel a booking from My Lab Tests until the sample has been collected. After the sample is collected, or the report is ready, a booking can no longer be cancelled in the app.

## Reports
The laboratory prepares your report and the booking status changes to report ready. Ask your doctor to explain your results; CareHub's assistant cannot interpret lab reports.
