# Surgery consultation requests

## What it is
You can ask a hospital for a consultation about a surgical procedure. Browse surgery hospitals in your city, choose the procedure and send a consultation request with your contact details. This is a request for a conversation, not a booked operation.

## What happens next
The hospital contacts you using the phone number you provided. Track your request under **My Surgery Requests**. Statuses are pending (waiting for the hospital), contacted, scheduled and closed.

## Costs
Procedures show an estimated cost range. Final costs depend on the hospital, the surgeon, your condition and the tests needed, and are confirmed by the hospital.

## Good to know
Do not include sensitive medical details in the notes field; discuss them directly with the hospital. For emergencies go to the nearest emergency department instead.
