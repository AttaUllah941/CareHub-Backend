# When to seek emergency care

## Call 1122 (Rescue) or go to the nearest emergency department immediately for
- Chest pain, pressure or tightness, especially with sweating, nausea or pain spreading to the arm or jaw.
- Difficulty breathing or being unable to speak in full sentences.
- Signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech, sudden confusion or loss of vision.
- Severe bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure.
- Loss of consciousness, a seizure, or a sudden severe headache.
- Suspected poisoning or an overdose.
- A severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat or difficulty breathing.
- Thoughts of harming yourself or someone else.

## How to prepare
Keep emergency numbers saved on your phone, know the nearest hospital with an emergency department, and keep a list of your medicines and allergies where family members can find it.

## Online tools are not for emergencies
CareHub appointments, video consultations and the AI assistant are not suitable for emergencies. If you are unsure whether a situation is an emergency, treat it as one and seek help in person.
