# CareHub account and doctor FAQ

## How do I create an account?
Choose Register, enter your name, email, phone number and a password of at least 8 characters, and sign in. Patients can then book appointments, lab tests and more.

## I forgot my password
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email. If an account exists you will receive a reset link that is valid for one hour. Using a reset link signs you out of all other devices.

## How do I join CareHub as a doctor?
Doctors do not register like patients. Use **Join as a doctor** and submit the application form with your personal details, specialty, qualifications, clinic details, consultation fee, availability and your documents (your medical licence, a profile photo and identification are required; a qualification certificate can also be added). The CareHub team reviews applications, usually within 2–3 business days. You can sign in once the application is approved; if it is not approved you will be told the reason.

## Are the doctors verified?
Only doctors who have been reviewed and approved by CareHub appear in search results and can receive bookings.

## How do I change my password?
Signed in, open the Change password page and enter your current password and a new one (at least 8 characters).

## Who do I contact for problems?
Use the contact details on the CareHub website footer. For urgent medical problems always seek care in person or call 1122.
