# Privacy and the CareHub AI assistant

## What the assistant can do
The CareHub AI assistant helps you find doctors, labs, medicines and hospitals, check availability, and prepare bookings and orders. It gives general health information only. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe medicines.

## You stay in control
Before the assistant books, cancels, orders or sends a request, it shows a summary card and nothing happens until you press **Confirm**. You can press Cancel instead. Confirmations expire after a short time.

## Your conversations
Your chats are private to your account and are never shown to other users. By default conversations are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically, and you can delete any conversation yourself from the chat history at any time. Avoid sharing more personal or medical detail than needed.

## Who can see what
The assistant for patients only accesses your own appointments, orders and requests. Doctors only see patients they have appointments with. Administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

## Emergencies
The assistant is not for emergencies. If you may be having an emergency, call 1122 or go to the nearest emergency department.
