# Booking and managing appointments on CareHub

## How booking works
Find a doctor, choose a clinic visit or a video consultation, pick a date and time slot and submit your request. You need to be signed in as a patient. Your request starts as **pending**, and the doctor then confirms or declines it. You are notified in the app and by email when the status changes.

## Statuses
Pending (waiting for the doctor), confirmed, completed, cancelled and rejected. If a doctor rejects a request, you will see their reason when one is given.

## Limits
You can have one active (pending or confirmed) appointment with the same doctor at a time. A time slot that is already taken cannot be booked again, so choose another time if you see a message that the slot is unavailable.

## Cancelling
You can cancel your own appointment while it is pending or confirmed from **My Appointments**. Completed, cancelled or rejected appointments cannot be changed.

## Video consultations
Once a video appointment is confirmed, a **Join video call** button appears for it in My Appointments. Use the same account you booked with and check your camera and microphone before the visit.

## Fees and payment
CareHub shows the doctor's consultation fee before you book. The app does not collect payment online at the moment, so fees are settled directly with the doctor or clinic.

## Reviews
After an appointment is marked completed you can leave one review for that doctor.
